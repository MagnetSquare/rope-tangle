Privacy Policy — Rope Tangle

Effective date: 23 October 2026
Developer: MagnetSquare Games
App: Rope Tangle (com.magnetsquare.ropetangle)

What this app does

Rope Tangle is a single-player puzzle game. You drag rope ends onto pins to untangle levels.

Data we collect

We do not collect, sell, or share personal data.

The game stores this only on your device:

  • Level progress and stars
  • Coin score
  • Music, sound, and vibration settings

That information never leaves the phone. There is no account, no cloud save, no advertising SDK, and no analytics SDK in this app.

Permissions

The app does not require internet, location, contacts, storage, camera, or microphone.

Vibration is optional and can be turned off in Settings.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect information from children because we do not collect personal information at all.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
